# Quick Hits Apps — Master Feature & Monetization Matrix
## Finalized Free vs. Pro Gating Logic

---

### 1. Historical Data & Analysis Dashboard
* **Archival Historical Data**: **Free** (App ships with foundational historical database out-of-the-box).
* **Live Syncing / Catch-Up**: Free users watch **1 Rewarded Video Ad** to sync newest live draws. Pro users sync automatically and instantly.

---

### 2. Search & Hit History (Year-over-Year Gating)
* **Current Year Search History**: **Free** for all users (instant search and hit history for the current year).
* **Past Years Hit History (Year-over-Year Browsing)**: **Pro Only** ($9.99 lifetime purchase unlocks browsing past years from 1992 to 2025).

---

### 3. Saved Favorites (Primary Monetization Driver)
* **Free Tier Limit**: Maximum of **3 saved favorite sets** (permanent local storage).
* **Pro Tier**: **Unlimited saved favorite sets** + ad-free experience. (Requires $9.99 lifetime purchase).

---

### 4. Smart Pick Generator & Custom Number Locking
* **Smart Pick Generator**: **Free** for all users.
* **Custom Number Locking**: **Free** for all users.

---

### 5. Manual Draw Entry
* **Manual Draw Entry**: **Free** for all users.

---

### 6. Advertisements & Monetization Summary
* **Free Tier**: Google AdMob Banner Ads + Rewarded Video Ad prompts for data syncing + Current year search history + 3 saved favorites limit.
* **Pro Tier ($9.99 Lifetime)**: **100% Ad-Free** + Past Years Search History (1992–2025) + Instant Background Syncing + Unlimited Favorites.
